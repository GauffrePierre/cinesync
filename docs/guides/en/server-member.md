# Getting started as a family member

Someone opened an account for you on their CinéSync. You have your own watchlist, ratings, services and settings: nobody else sees them. About 5 minutes.

Open the address you were given (it starts with `https://`), then follow your case.

## With a Plex account

The administrator allowed your Plex account.

1. On the **Sign in** screen, choose **Sign in with Plex**.
2. A 4-character code appears: go to [plex.tv/link](https://plex.tv/link), sign in with your usual Plex account and enter the code. (Or use "open the Plex sign-in window".)
3. Back in CinéSync, you're signed in. Your Plex watched titles and progress are your own.

If you were also given a temporary password, or want to sign in without Plex: **Profile → My account → Create a password**.

## Without a Plex account

The administrator gave you a **username** and a **temporary password**.

1. On the **Sign in** screen, enter them and choose **Sign in**.
2. Choose your own password (at least 8 characters): you're asked on first sign-in.

## Then, for everyone

In **Profile**:

1. **Region & Language**: your country, content language and interface language.
2. **Platforms**: tick your subscriptions (badges, filters, "Watch on…", notifications).
3. **Cinemas**: the cinemas you go to.
4. **Connections → +**: link **your** Trakt account (**Sign in with Trakt**, code at trakt.tv/activate) and/or your Letterboxd, if you have them.
5. **Sync**: **Sync now** gathers your watchlist, watched titles and ratings.
6. **Notifications**: choose what shows under the bell.

### Request a film or series
On the page of a title that isn't in the library: **Request it**. Depending on the administrator's settings, the request goes straight through, or they receive and handle it; you're notified either way. Track your requests in **Profile → My requests**.

### Install the app on your phone
- **iPhone / iPad**: Safari → **Share → Add to Home Screen**. "Play on Plex" will offer to open the Plex app.
- **Android**: Chrome offers to install it; "Watch on…" links open the installed apps directly.

---
Can't sign in? Ask the administrator to check your account (Admin area → Users).
